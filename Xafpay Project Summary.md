               Xafpay Project Summary
What is Xafpay?
Xafpay is a platform that helps people in the USA and Canada send money to Cameroon quickly and securely. It also includes a wallet system where users can store money before sending it.

I. Key Features (MVP)
  1- User Onboarding
    - Sign up, log in, reset password
    - Block users from restricted countries
    - Verify identity (KYC)
    
  2- Wallet & Money Management
    - Add money via Plaid Connect (link bank account)
    - Check balance
    - Send money to Cameroon

  3- Security & Compliance
    - 2FA (Biometrics/OTP) for secure transactions
    - Embargo checks (block restricted users)

  4- Payouts to Cameroon
    - Send money via MTN or Orange mobile money
    - Option for cash pickup
    
  5- Future Plans
    - Multi-currency wallets (USD, CAD, XAF)
    - Pay bills, invest, merchant payments
    - Bulk transfers (send to many people at once)

II. How It Works
  1- User signs up → Verifies identity (KYC)
  2- Connects bank account (via Plaid)
  3- Adds money to wallet
  4- Sends money to Cameroon (mobile money or cash pickup)

III. Vision
  1- Expand to more countries & currencies
  2- Offer crypto (XAF-backed coin)
  3- Become a payment solution for businesses

Goal: Make sending money to Cameroon fast, easy, and affordable.
